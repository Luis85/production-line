# Historical evidence archive

These files record past implementations, investigations and verification runs.
Their screenshots intentionally show the UI at that time. Do not refresh or
relabel them as current product imagery. Use [current screenshots](../readme/README.md)
and [current documentation](../../index.md) for the released workflow.

- [Review history](../../reports/index.md) explains the dated records.
- [Worker UI goals](../../reports/worker-ui-goals-2026-10-26.md) records the focused-view review.
- [Home qualification](werk-home-2026-10-26/README.md) retains archive/Git/runtime evidence.
- [Workflow dashboard](workflow-dashboard-2026-10-26/README.md) records flow and coverage observations.
- [Workflow mutation follow-up](local-delivery/mutation-workflow-v2-followup-2026-10-25/README.md) and [dashboard mutation follow-up](local-delivery/mutation-dashboard-v1-followup-2026-10-26/README.md) retain raw measurements and residual findings.

The [cleanup receipt](cleanup-2026-10-26.json) records removed unreferenced images,
SHA-256 hashes, archive moves and their recovery commit. Linked captures, explicit
before/after comparisons, validation receipts and raw mutation reports are retained.
Unreferenced PNGs remain recoverable from [the pre-cleanup source](https://github.com/Luis85/production-line/tree/46682ee5c23e133731f1f37477d550925073f877/projects/werk/docs/evidence).
