These reports record work and evidence at a particular point in time. Their test
counts, environment limits and remaining gaps do not override the
[current documentation](../index.md). Imported handoff material is retained
separately under `docs/handoff`; original source provenance is recorded in the
[concept import note](../CONCEPT.md).
